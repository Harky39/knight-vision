Questions

Does Knight Vision work with Chess.com?

Yes. The extension is designed for Chess.com game, puzzle and analysis pages.

What is the Free limit?

The current Free tier includes 50 analyses. After the limit is reached, PRO unlocks unlimited solving.

What does PRO change?

PRO removes the solve limit and raises the configured Stockfish search depth from 15 to 25.

How are payments handled?

Knight Vision uses ExtensionPay with Stripe to manage paid access. Payment handling is kept outside the extension itself.
